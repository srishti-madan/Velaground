04 / Velasphere · Deep dive

Pressed once. Reads for 90 days. Dissolves completely.

The Velasphere sits in the superficial venous network of the inner wrist and reads your hormonal and environmental markers continuously for one quarter. No charging. No removal. No daily decision.

Stage 01 · 30 seconds
01 · The press

Hold for thirty seconds.

Place the 8mm sphere against the inner wrist and apply gentle pressure. It is painless. There is no needle, no incision, no skin damage.

Once per quarter No equipment
Stage 02 · Migration
02 · Migration

Into the superficial venous network.

The sphere migrates beneath the surface into the small capillary network just below the skin. It does not enter the bloodstream. It does not move.

Localised Non-systemic
Stage 03 · 90 days of reading
03 · Ninety days

It reads. You receive.

For 90 days the sphere continuously measures hormonal markers, pollutant levels, and environmental exposure. Data flows to your app in real time. At day 90 it dissolves naturally, leaving only a faint silver trace along the veins. Dissolution can be triggered earlier through the app.
